What is on every card

Quality and price are kept apart, so a good company is never confused with a good price.

Q

Quality grade

Team, asset, jurisdiction, financial strength and growth, scored only from facts we could source. Missing data lowers confidence; it is never guessed.

V

Valuation grade

Priced against its own stage: net asset value for developers, free cash flow for producers. Normalised to a common metal price deck and stress-tested.

✓

Project scorecard

24 yes/no tests of project readiness, from grade and mine life to permits, partners and funding. Unknown never counts as a fail.

How we keep it honest

Every input has a source

Each figure carries a verbatim quote, the document and the page. Click through to see exactly where it came from.

Two readings, then a check

Documents are read twice independently. Figures that disagree, or fail an arithmetic or range check, are held for a person. Only verified facts reach a published card.

Disclosed, always

If the site owner holds a position or a company sponsors the site, the card says so at the top. Sponsorship never changes a grade.

Coverage today: companies that file with the US SEC. Canadian and Australian-only filers are not covered yet. Read the full method.
